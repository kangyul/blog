Hi, I'm Yul.

I'm learning computer graphics from the ground up — the math, the C++, and the
rendering pipeline — and writing down what I figure out along the way. This blog
is where those notes end up once they're coherent enough to be useful to someone
other than me.

## What I write about

- **Graphics fundamentals** — the pipeline, coordinate spaces, shaders, and the
  linear algebra underneath them
- **C++** — the parts that matter for real rendering code
- **MapLibre Native** — reading and contributing to a production renderer
